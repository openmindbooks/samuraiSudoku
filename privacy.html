Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “The Samurai Way: Extreme Sudoku” (package com.openmindbooks.samuraisudoku) and the Samurai Sudoku website.
Publisher: Open Mind Books · Last updated: 21 July 2026

In short: this is a Sudoku game. It does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your game progress is stored only on your device. It shows no ads and does not sell your data.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following are saved locally on your device (in the app’s private storage) and are not transmitted to us or to any server:

  • Your puzzle progress (board entries and pencil marks)
  • Which puzzles you have solved, and your daily play streak
  • Counters used to decide when to ask you to rate the app
  • A flag recording whether you have purchased the “unlock all puzzles” option

You can erase all of this at any time from Menu → Settings → Clear progress, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Purchases

The app offers a single one-time in-app purchase (“unlock all puzzles”). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The app never sees or stores your payment card, billing address, or Google account details. Google tells the app only whether the purchase succeeded, and the app then stores a local flag so your puzzles stay unlocked offline. Google’s handling of the transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics

The app may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used so we can improve it. When enabled, it collects anonymous, aggregated information only:

  • Which screens are opened and in-game events (for example: a game started, a game finished, a hint used, a purchase completed)
  • A randomly generated installation identifier used to count unique installs (not linked to your identity)
  • Basic device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version) and general region estimated by Google from your IP address. We do not store your IP address.

It does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account
  • The Android Advertising ID or any advertising identifier (advertising-ID collection is disabled in the app)
  • Your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera

Analytics data is processed by Google on our behalf under the Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase terms.

4. Advertising

The app contains no advertising and uses no ad networks.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. The only third parties involved are Google (for payment processing and, if enabled, analytics), acting as our service providers. On-device data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Aggregated analytics data is retained by Google according to its standard retention settings.

8. Your choices

  • Clear all on-device data: Menu → Settings → Clear progress, or uninstall.
  • Request information about data associated with this app: contact us below.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date.
